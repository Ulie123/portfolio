Gambit HallChess analysis & coaching engine

A full chess coach that runs entirely in the browser — no server, no account, no network calls. It evaluates positions, explains every recommendation in plain language, and teaches through a curriculum I wrote from scratch.

  • Alpha-beta search with a custom tapered evaluation function
  • Rule-based explanation engine — what a move attacks, defends and prevents, drawn as board arrows (no LLM involved)
  • PGN / FEN import, auto-analysis, move grading, configurable AI opponents
  • Progressive curriculum from beginner through ~2000 rating

PROJECT / 02

The ArcadeFour classic games, ported & rebuilt

A browser arcade of four games. Three began life as Java Swing applications and were ported to vanilla JavaScript with their original logic intact; the fourth was built for the web from the start.

  • Fianchetto (chess) — full legal move generation including castling, en passant and promotion, against a CPU tuned to roughly 800 ELO
  • Othello — two opponents: a greedy heuristic and a minimax AI searching six moves deep
  • Tic-Tac-Toe — three interchangeable interfaces (buttons, hand-painted canvas, text console) swappable mid-game
  • Pong — solo and versus-CPU, with trajectory derived from paddle contact point
